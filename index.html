FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

About Fluxbeam, Token-2022, and the Solana ecosystem

What is Fluxbeam? +

Fluxbeam is the first SPL22-enabled DEX on Solana, built around the Token-2022 standard. It supports tokens with transfer fees, interest-bearing balances, confidential transfers, and other extensions.

What is Token-2022 (SPL22)? +

Token-2022 is Solana's next-generation token standard with built-in extensions like transfer fees, interest-bearing balances, confidential transfers, and non-transferability. Fluxbeam is the first DEX to fully support these features.

Can I launch a Token-2022 token on Fluxbeam? +

Yes. Fluxbeam has a built-in token launcher that lets you deploy Token-2022 assets with configurable extensions in a few clicks — no coding required.

What is the $FLUX token? +

$FLUX is the native token of the Fluxbeam ecosystem. It's used for governance, fee discounts, and community rewards. The token has seen significant growth with over 440% increase in 24h.

Is Fluxbeam non-custodial? +

Yes. Fluxbeam is a non-custodial DEX — your funds remain in your wallet at all times. Smart contracts execute trades directly from your wallet.

What wallets does Fluxbeam support? +

Fluxbeam supports all major Solana wallets including Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Glow, and any wallet compatible with the Solana Wallet Adapter.

Does Fluxbeam have developer tools? +

Yes. Fluxbeam offers a comprehensive toolkit including the Flux Aggregator API, token creation tools, pool management, token locker, fee manager, and token migration tools.

How do I get started? +

Connect your Solana wallet, explore the swap interface, provide liquidity, or launch your own Token-2022 token. Full documentation is available at docs.fluxbeam.xyz.
